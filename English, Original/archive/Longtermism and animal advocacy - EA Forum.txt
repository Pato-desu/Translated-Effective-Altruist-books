It is vital to avoid any actions that could impair our ability to achieveour long-term goals (as individuals, as organisations, and as a movement).Maximizing the likelihood of eventually achieving su"cient concern for allsentient beings could be much more important than accelerating the process.In particular, one way to jeopardize our long-term inﬂuence is by triggering aserious (and permanent) backlash, so we should take reasonable steps to preventthe movement from becoming too controversial.
