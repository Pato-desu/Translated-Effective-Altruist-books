SCOTT ALEXANDERAskReddit asked recently: If you could only give an alien one thing tohelp them understand the human race, what would you give them?At the time I had no good answer. Now I do. I would give them CharlotteLennox’s write-up of how MsScribe took over Harry Potter fandom(warning: super-long but super-worth-it).Ozy informs me that everyone else in the world read this story five yearsago. Maybe I am hopelessly behind the times? Maybe all my blog readersare intimately familiar with it?If not, read it. Read it like an anthropological text. Read it like you woulda study of the Yanomamo. No, read it even better than that. Read it likeyou would a study of the Yanomamo if you knew that, statistically, someof your friends and co-workers covertly become Yanomamo after gettinghome every evening.I hesitate to summarize it, because people will read my summary andignore the much superior original. I would not recommend that.
