ACE Highlight: Volunteering Advice
February 18, 2015
by
Jon Bockman
- 1 minute read
This blog post is a part of our
ACE Highlight series
, where we feature current, relevant, and engaging topics from the ACE website.
We have recently updated our page on
Volunteering Advice
to feature a more digestible format so that readers can get the information they need in a quick and easy manner.
Instead of listing all of our advice in a long document, we’ve broken down our thoughts on volunteering into various sections based on each animal cause. Our
main page
explains our thinking on recommending farm animal charities as well as broad overall considerations. It also offers comparisons of the impact you could have by volunteering for various animal causes, including farm animal advocacy. Finally, it contains a link to our page on
ways to volunteer for farmed animals
.
This reorganization serves multiple purposes. In addition to allowing readers to more quickly navigate to their point of interest, it also allows us to better target people through Google AdWords. The latter is a particularly important point, as we’ve recently been approved for an increase in our monthly Google
grant for nonprofits
.
What do you think of our volunteering advice pages? Let us know in the comments below.
