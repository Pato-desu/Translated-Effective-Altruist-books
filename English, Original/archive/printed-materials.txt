ACE Highlight: Printed Materials
October 27, 2014
by
Erika Alonso
- 1 minute read
This blog post is a part of our
ACE Highlight series
, where we feature current, relevant, and engaging topics from the ACE website.
To effectively promote our work and help raise money for our recommended charities, it’s important to develop professional materials for distribution. To that end, we created two booklets that we used at conferences this summer, the
ACE trifold
and our
Guide To Giving
. The trifold serves as an introduction to our work and what we believe, while the Guide To Giving offers a concise way for donors to understand and support our recommendations. Please feel free to share these documents with those unfamiliar with ACE’s work, as they are a great introduction to our organization.
Did we do a good job explaining our work? Is there something we can improve? Let us know in the comments below.
