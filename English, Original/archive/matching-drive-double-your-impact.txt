Matching Drive – Double Your Impact!
September 18, 2014
by
Jon Bockman
- 1 minute read
*Update October 16, 2014: Thanks to a generous donor, the matching funds amount has increased to $40,000.
I’m excited to announce that a generous donor has requested a
matching drive
for ACE! This means that every donation to ACE through November 19 will truly be doubled up to $25,000. If you’re concerned with maximizing your donation, now is the perfect time to support our work!
Any donation you make at this time will be doubled, but there’s a way where you could raise even more money for ACE: By creating a page that you can share with your friends and family, you can easily keep track of the funds you raise up to that point. You can even engage in some friendly competition with other generous donors to try and raise the most money for ACE.
Additionally, you can use our
conventional donate page
to give via check or to arrange a bank transfer. Regardless of how you donate during this campaign, your donation will be doubled—it’s as simple as that!
Thank you for supporting our
genuine matching drive
. Through your support, we can work together to save as many animals as possible.
