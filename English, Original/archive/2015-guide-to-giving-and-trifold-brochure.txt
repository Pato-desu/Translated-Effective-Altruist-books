2015 Guide to Giving and Trifold Brochure
April 20, 2015
by
Erika Alonso
- 1 minute read
Note: Our estimate of “animals spared” used in our printed materials was not optimally updated to our reflect our latest available data. We
acknowledge this mistake
, but don’t believe it significantly affects our overall message and thus will continue to use these materials for the duration of 2015.
Our trifold brochure and Guide to Giving were well received last year, so we’ve updated new versions to reflect our updated recommendations. We invite you to review them at the links below.
We’ll be featuring these materials at several VegFests and at the annual AR conference. If you know about an event where you think our literature would be a perfect fit, please
contact us
and we’ll do our best to meet your needs. Also, we encourage you to share these on social media to help spread the word about the work we are doing to help animals.
