The way to order and remember longvisual stories is to use a memory palace(duh). So, now what I do is I have a 'main'palace (like a friend's house). Every placewithin this palace encodes two things: theequation number I'm trying to memorize,and a link to another palace (this issurprisingly easy. I don't need to doanything special to encode the link,weirdly.) In that linked palace, I divide upthe equation and place the visual elementsin some familiar route.I thought I would have trouble coming upwith enough palaces, but it hasn't been anissue so far. I also re-use the palaces, andas long as the context between the uses ofthe palace are dissimilar, it doesn't appearto be a problem.Soo, what?I mean, it works.
