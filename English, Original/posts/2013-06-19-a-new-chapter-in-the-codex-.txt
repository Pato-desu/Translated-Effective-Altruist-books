SCOTT ALEXANDERFor the past year, I have been living off of some very occasional part-time work for MetaMed, some odd jobs, and savings from varioussources. It hasn’t been the most lucrative thing in the world, but it’s leftme lots of time for thinking and writing.Tomorrow I start Real Work. It’s just training for the first couple of weeksand I may have a bit of time, but after that I have been told it is not thesort of thing that leaves much time for thinking and writing (or eating, orsleeping). I am not going to keep up blogging once per day. Maybe I willbe able to blog once a week? A couple of times a month?Because I will be working on medicine and psychiatry full time, I expectthis to turn into more of a Medicine And Psychiatry Blog than it is rightnow.
