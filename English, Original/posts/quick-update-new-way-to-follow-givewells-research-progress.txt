There are two types of materials we publish periodically throughout the year:
We frequently speak with charity representatives or other subject matter experts. We ask permission to take notes during these conversations so that we can publish them to our
conversations page
.
We publish new charity review or intervention report pages.
We’ve set up a Google Group so that those who want can get updated when we publish new material.
You can subscribe to this via RSS using
this RSS feed
. You can also sign up to receive updates via email at the
group’s home page
.
