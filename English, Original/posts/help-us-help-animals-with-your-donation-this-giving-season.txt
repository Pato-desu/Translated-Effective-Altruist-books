Help us help animals with your donation this giving season
December 23, 2013
by
Jon Bockman
- 1 minute read
As many of you know, we’ll be rebranding as Animal Charity Evaluators before the end of the year. Our new site featuring additional content will launch on December 27. We are writing to ask for your
support
to continue finding and promoting the best ways to help animals. To maintain operations through 2014 with our current budget,
we need to raise about $60,000.
If we could secure enough money to expand,
our next priority would be to hire an additional Researcher.
We have decided to focus the coming year on research/analysis and refining our recommendations. Providing a solid foundation for our
recommendations
will empower activists and organizations to confidently support effective animal advocacy. Some of the progress we’ve made to date:
We conducted two impactful studies in fall 2013, and we look forward to sharing the results of our
leafleting study
in January and our
humane education study
in March.
We recently hired a full-time Research Manager, and several outstanding interns and volunteers have joined
our team
.
We have composed a list of prospective goals and planned research that will be featured on our new site, along with new solid criteria for evaluating organizations and interventions.
We’re happy for people to wait and check out our new site (launching Dec. 27) before giving. Any
donation
made in the United States by December 31st will qualify as a tax-deduction in 2013, and we have
tax-deductible options in other countries
as well. Thank you in advance for
your support
. It is only through a collective effort that we can promote effective interventions and charities to activists and donors around the world.
Sincerely,
Jon

About Jon Bockman
Jon has held diverse leadership positions in nonprofit animal advocacy over the past decade. His career prior to ACE included serving as a Director at a shelter and wildlife rehabilitation center, as a humane investigator, and as a Founder of a 501(c)3 farm animal advocacy group.
