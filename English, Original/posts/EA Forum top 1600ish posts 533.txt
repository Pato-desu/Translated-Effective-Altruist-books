Most sources are things I quick-ly googled so are probably off themark.TL;DR It’s sometimes claimed or suggested that if an extinctionevent were to happen, it would happen very quickly (i.e. days,weeks, months). However, when empires fall or species go ex-tinct, they usually do so very slowly (years, decades, millennia).These base rates suggest we should have a reasonably strongprior on slow decline when thinking about civilisational collapseand, at more of a stretch, existential risks.
