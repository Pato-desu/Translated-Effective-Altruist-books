I con-clude that the risk of phosphorous depletion to global food secu-rity are minimal.***‘Peak phosphorous’ is one of the more popular ‘peak resource’theories, along with ‘peak oil’.[1] Phosphorous is essential forfood production, and has no substitutes. Organic sources of phos-phorous, such as crop residues, food waste, excreta, are classedas renewable. Phosphate rock on the other hand is classed asnon-renewable and finite because it cycles from the lithosphereto the hydrosphere at rates of millions of years.
