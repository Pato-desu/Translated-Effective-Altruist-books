In medicaljargon, healthy people are “alert and oriented x 3”, which meansoriented to person (you know your name), oriented to time (you knowwhat day/month/year it is), and oriented to place (you know you’re in ahospital). My patients who have the sorts of issues I mentioned in thelast paragraph are generally alert and oriented x0. There are a lot of people screaming “SOMEBODY HELP ME!” Andthere are some religious screams, like “OH GOD!” or “JESUS HELP ME!”or “CHRIST NO!”.When I first started working in hospitals, I would not only inevitably runover to these screams, but I would feel contempt and anger at the restof the hospital staff who would just continue their daily routine.
