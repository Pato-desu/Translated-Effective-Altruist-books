But whatever the case,there is one question that these people need toget right: "Is the AI we're about to deploysafe?"For people to get this answer right when itmatters, two things need to happen: we needtools to accurately determine whether anadvanced AI is safe, and we need an advancedAI those tools approve of that we can deploy intime for it to matter.Training stories are a tool to evaluate proposalsfor making this happen. The reason to focus on completeproposals is that it gets us to pay attention towhat will be important when push comes toshove.
