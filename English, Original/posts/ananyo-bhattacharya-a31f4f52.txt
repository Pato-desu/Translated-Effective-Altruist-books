Ananyo Bhattacharya is the author of
The Man from the Future: The Visionary Life of John von Neumann
. He is a science writer who has worked at the Economist and Nature. Before journalism, he was a medical researcher at the Burnham Institute in San Diego, California. He holds a degree in physics from the University of Oxford and a PhD in protein crystallography from Imperial College London.
