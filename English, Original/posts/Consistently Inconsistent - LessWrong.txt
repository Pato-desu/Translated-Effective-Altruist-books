Now one asks, what did ”the patient” think was going on? A crucial point of the bookis that there's no such thing as the patient. ”3e patient” is just two di1erenthemispheres, to some extent disconnected. Does someone with a phantomlimb ”believe” that all of their limbs are intact? If you ask them, they'll say no, butnonetheless they feel pain in their missing limb.
