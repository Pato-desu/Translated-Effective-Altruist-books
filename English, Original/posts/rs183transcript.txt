experience that changes what you know about yourself, or about the world,

change about the way you think about yourself, or it causes a change in the 
way you think about yourself or understand yourself or in who you are.
The epistemic transformation, which is this change or sort of mental 
discovery that you make, then brings about a personal change, and it's a 
personal change that's not just minimal. It's not like getting your hair cut, 
but it changes what you care about, how you think of yourself or who you 
care about.
